# Funebra Arts

Practice inside Funebra™ AI-Oriented Academia.

> Observe. Construct. Test. Document. Remain human.

## Invitation

Master the Funebra Arts.

Learn to observe before interpreting, construct before claiming, test what can be tested, document what happened, and keep human judgment in control.

Funebra Arts is the practice inside Funebra™ AI-Oriented Academia: art, mathematics, making, language, sound, and human-directed AI in one inspectable chain. The work demonstrates skill. No title is conferred.

## Definition

Funebra Arts names the practical discipline of moving deliberately through:

Observation → Question → Construction → AI-assisted test → Evidence → Reflection → Publication

Academia is the institution. Funebra Arts is the practice. The Founding Charter is the governing standard. The Founding Demonstration is the first inspectable example.

Math-Art, Eye27, Alpha-Lughaic, sound, and physical making may later appear as practices under Funebra Arts. They are not courses and not qualifications.

## Three fences

No degree, licence, or membership is granted. “Master” describes continued practice, not a title. The Founding Charter governs Funebra Arts: Observation ≠ Interpretation ≠ Proof.
